About Us

Established in 1989, we are the oldest computer-based organization at BINUS University. For 36 years, we have grown into a community built on professionalism and a strong culture of continuous learning. We believe in collaboration and the potential of every individual. We create a supportive environment where people can explore technology, develop leadership skills, and grow through real experiences. Our goal is to help each person grow with purpose and create meaningful impact in the world of technology.
